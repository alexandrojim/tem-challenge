## Evaluation & Verification Policy

* **Replicability Check:** Before official winners are confirmed, the organizing committee will verify that the top-scoring teams' submitted `/code` repositories and model weights reproduce their submitted `/predictions` instance masks on the held-out test micrographs.
* **Tie-Breaking Rule:** Overall ranking is determined by the aggregate rank across $\text{DSC}_{\text{Mean}}$, $\text{HD}_{95\text{, Mean}}$, and $g\text{-Ratio MAE}$. In the event of a tie, the submission with the lowest physical boundary error ($\text{HD}_{95\text{, Mean}}$) will be ranked higher.
* **Award & Presentation Requirement:** While challenge participation is open to all researchers and students worldwide, at least one member of the winning team must be registered for the **SIPAIM Main Conference (November 18–20, 2026)** or the **SIPAIM Workshop (November 16, 2026)** to officially claim the award and present their methodology during the challenge session.
